Spinning Vinyl Record & Center Artwork Visualizer

Showcase singles, lo-fi beats, and album previews with an animated turntable or circular spectrum wrapped around your own cover art. audvis supports custom center image uploads across Vinyl Record, Radial Bars, Polar Wave, and Neon Rings.

How to Add Your Album Cover

  1. Launch the studio with Vinyl Record or Radial Bars selected.
  2. Load your audio file via Open Audio or drag-and-drop.
  3. In the Style & Stage panel, click Upload Center Art and select any square or landscape image — it automatically crops into a clean spinning center label.
  4. Adjust Radial Scale for circular bar reach, then export in 1:1 or 16:9 as WebM or MP4.
