Does audvis upload my audio files to a server?

No. audvis runs entirely locally inside your browser using the Web Audio API and HTML5 Canvas, so your audio files and microphone recordings never leave your device.

Can I export an audio visualizer with a transparent background?

Yes. Set Stage Background to Transparent and Export Format to WebM (VP9) to render a video with an alpha channel for video editor overlays.

What audio visualizer styles does audvis include?

audvis includes 18 visualizers: Frequency Bars, Mirror Bars, Spectrogram, Filled Wave, Oscilloscope, Siri Ribbons, Mountain Ridge, Aurora Curtains, Radial Bars, Polar Wave, Neon Rings, Organic Blob, Vinyl Record, Hexagon Grid, Cyber Grid, Audio Helix, Particle Vortex, and Dot to Dot.

Should I export WebM or MP4?

Choose WebM (VP9) when you need transparent backgrounds and smaller overlay files. Choose MP4 when importing directly into Premiere Pro, Final Cut Pro, or Safari (MP4 uses an opaque backdrop).
