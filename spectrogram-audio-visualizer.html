Inspect harmonics, transients, and frequency balance over time with audvis. Switch between the scrolling Spectrogram waterfall, high-detail Frequency Bars, and time-domain Oscilloscope from any audio file or live Mic input.

DSP Controls for Frequency Inspection

  • FFT Detail (32 – 4096): Increase FFT size to resolve narrow bass harmonics and vocal formants.
  • Smoothing (0.00 – 0.95): Lower smoothing to 0.15–0.40 to catch sharp drum transients, or raise it for smooth visual averages.
  • Live Mic Capture: Click Mic in the top bar to inspect instruments or room acoustics without speaker feedback.
