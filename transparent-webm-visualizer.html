Transparent WebM Audio Visualizer for Video Overlays

Need an animated soundwave or spectrum overlay you can drop straight onto B-roll, podcast footage, or a livestream scene? audvis exports alpha-transparent WebM (VP9) videos directly from an HTML5 Canvas without uploading your audio anywhere.

How to Export a Transparent Soundwave Overlay

  1. Open your audio track (MP3, WAV, AAC, or OGG) or test with the built-in Demo Synth.
  2. Under Style & Stage, keep Stage Background set to Transparent (you will see the subtle checkerboard backdrop).
  3. Pick any of the 18 visual engines — Frequency Bars, Mirror Bars, Siri Ribbons, Radial Bars, or Polar Wave work especially well for overlays.
  4. Under Video Export Studio, choose WebM (VP9 • Supports Transparency), set Resolution to 2× Crisp or 3× Ultra, and click Export WEBM.

Why audvis WebM Exports Scrub Cleanly

Standard browser MediaRecorder streams often omit duration metadata in the WebM container, making seeking broken in editors. audvis runs a client-side EBML metadata patcher (fixWebmDuration) before download so your exported file scrubs accurately in DaVinci Resolve, OBS, CapCut, and modern NLEs.
